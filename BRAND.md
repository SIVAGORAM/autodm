# HeyLaunch — Brand & Product Vision

**Finalized brand identity.** All product design, engineering, content, and marketing should align with this.

## Name & Positioning
- **Brand:** HeyLaunch
- **Domain:** heylaunch.app
- **Tagline:** Start Conversations. Launch Possibilities.
- **Website headline:** Your audience. Your presence. Your growth.
- **Short description:** HeyLaunch helps creators and businesses build their online presence, automate supported social interactions, manage conversations, and grow their audience from one platform.

**Meaning:** *Hey* = connection, communication, engagement (the start of a conversation). *Launch* = create, publish, automate, grow (taking a brand/presence to the next level).

## What HeyLaunch Is
Not just an Instagram DM tool — a unified digital-growth platform where creators and businesses establish their online identity, connect with their audience, automate engagement, and manage their digital presence from one place.

**Product areas (vision; not all built yet):**
- Instagram Automation — comment-to-DM, keyword triggers, approved automation (official Meta API only)
- Public Creator Profiles — `heylaunch.app/username`
- Link-in-Bio
- Lead Capture & Engagement
- Unified Inbox
- Analytics
- Future: creator tools, integrations, AI-assisted workflows

**Proposed module names:** HeyLaunch Automations, Profiles, Links, Inbox, Leads, Analytics, Workflows.

## Audience
Content creators, influencers, entrepreneurs, SMBs, digital marketers, agencies, personal brands, online service providers. Approachable for beginners, powerful for pros.

## Brand Personality
Friendly · Professional · Modern · Powerful · Scalable. Communication: clear, confident, simple, helpful. Avoid jargon and exaggerated claims.

## Visual Identity
- **Logo:** four-petal gradient symbol (purple + lavender + signature lime) with the HeyLaunch wordmark. Finalized — do not redesign without approval. Files: `frontend/public/heylaunch-logo.png` (wordmark), `frontend/public/heylaunch-icon.png` (symbol), `frontend/app/icon.png` (favicon).
- **App UI:** Premium Black + White + Charcoal + Signature Lime. Keep surfaces neutral so the colorful logo stands out. Support light + dark themes. Avoid purple-heavy dashboards, excessive gradients, neon surfaces, and decoration.

## Engineering Guardrails
1. HeyLaunch is a multi-feature SaaS platform, not merely an Instagram DM bot.
2. Preserve the brand name and approved logo.
3. Follow the black/white/charcoal + lime UI system.
4. Keep UX simple, professional, accessible.
5. Design for future expansion.
6. Treat creator profiles / public pages as first-class capabilities.
7. Preserve existing functionality and security.
8. Follow platform API policies and permissions.
9. Do not invent product capabilities, customer stats, or business claims.
10. Keep identity consistent across website, dashboard, public profiles, and future modules.
