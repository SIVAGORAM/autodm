# Brand — AutoDM clone

Grounds the name, voice and look in the positioning from `fixes.md`:
**reliability + transparency — "know it's working."** Not cheapest, not
flashiest. Dependable. Built on the teal/coral design system in `design/`.

> "AutoDM" is the generic category + the current working title. Pick a real name
> below before launch, and verify the domain + trademark in your country.

## Naming criteria
- Says *dependable*, not *cheap*.
- One or two syllables, easy to say in English and Hindi/Telugu.
- Ownable: a .com or .in you can actually get; clears a basic trademark search.
- Never contains "SuperProfile", "ManyChat" or a competitor's name.

## Candidates

| name | meaning / vibe | notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relayo** ★ | "relay" + friendly -o: your DMs relayed, reliably | easy to say; coined, so likely ownable (check relayo.in / getrelayo.com) |
| Sureply | "sure" + "reply": reliability baked in | strong meaning; risk of mis-reading as "sure-ply" |
| DMPulse | a heartbeat that proves the automation is alive | on-theme (health/status); "Pulse" is crowded |
| Dispatch | logistics-grade "it gets sent" | confident; common word, harder to own |

## Recommendation: **Relayo**
Easy to say, clearly means "it relays your DMs," friendly for a creator audience,
and coined enough to actually own. It lets the *reliability* story live in the
tagline and the product (status + delivery log), instead of forcing a clunky
descriptive name.

- **Tagline:** "Know it's working."
- **Secondary line (pricing proof):** "Reliable auto-DMs. One flat price. No contact caps."

## Wordmark / logo direction
- Wordmark in teal (`--primary`), lowercase `relayo`, medium weight, tight tracking.
- Mark: a simple **signal/relay dot** — a filled teal dot with one concentric
  ring (a "live" pulse), or a small paper-plane turning into a check. Coral used
  only for the "live" accent dot, never the whole logo.
- Favicon: the teal dot + ring.
- Keep it flat (no gradients) to match the design system.

## Brand voice
Dependable, plain, a little warm. You're the tool a creator trusts with their
viral moment.
- **Do:** short sentences. Say what happened ("12 DMs sent, 0 failed"). Reassure
  ("You'll know the second anything breaks."). Indian-creator-friendly, not
  corporate.
- **Don't:** hype, "revolutionary", fake urgency, emoji spam, or leaning on
  "cheapest." Cheap invites a cheaper competitor; reliable doesn't.
- Error/empty states carry the brand hardest: a clear "Needs reconnect — tap to
  fix" beats a red stack trace. This is where "know it's working" is felt.

## How it uses the design system (`design/`)
- Teal `--primary` = the brand. Coral `--accent` = the one action per screen
  (Publish / Reconnect) and the "live" status dot.
- Status language maps to tokens: Healthy = success (teal-green), Needs
  reconnect = warning (amber), Paused by Meta = muted.
- The delivery log and status badges ARE the brand's proof — style them crisp.
