# Feedback analysis — what creators hate about Instagram auto-DM tools

Sample: a representative read, not a full census. ManyChat is the incumbent and
carries almost all the public complaint volume; SuperProfile (the app being
cloned) is smaller and better-liked, with a thin public review base. Sources:
review aggregators (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot roll-ups), ManyChat's own community
forum (15+ distinct "stopped working" threads, 2024–2026), and SuperProfile's
Trustpilot + help docs. Full rows with links in `reviews.csv`.

## Themes, ranked (weighted to low ratings + recency)

### 1. Reliability — automations silently stop working  ★ strongest, cross-product
By far the loudest theme. Dozens of ManyChat community threads: automations show
LIVE but stop firing, often for days, with no alert. Real cost to creators — a
burned lead magnet on a viral post.
- Evidence: 15+ ManyChat forum threads (2024–2026); SuperProfile's own doc
  admits automations stop and the account must be reconnected.
- Quotes: "I am losing followers at this point. This is causing horrible stress."
  (ManyChat forum); "When someone comments the keyword, absolutely nothing
  happens. No DM, no trigger, nothing." (InstantDM blog, quoting a user).
- Why it happens: Meta token/permission expiry + webhook gaps, and **no status
  visibility** so the creator finds out only when leads dry up.

### 2. Pricing that punishes growth  ★ strong (ManyChat-specific)
ManyChat bills by active contacts; a viral reel spikes the bill. The #1 reason
people search for alternatives. (SuperProfile already counters this with no
contact cap — so this is a wedge vs ManyChat, table stakes vs SuperProfile.)
- Evidence: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Reddit, multiple alternative round-ups.
- Quotes: "the bill grows with your audience" (Tidio); "Their pricing structure
  and not enough selections it's all or nothing" (G2).

### 3. Support unresponsive  ★ strong (ManyChat)
When the tool breaks (theme 1), support is slow or unreachable — the two
compound into the Trustpilot 2.1.
- Evidence: Trustpilot 2.1/~306; forum reports the Get-help form itself is broken.
- Quote: "the Get help button and support request form are not working for me."

### 4. Builder too complex for Instagram-only creators  ★ medium
ManyChat's flow builder is overkill for "comment → DM a link." Creators want
one simple job done well.
- Evidence: multiple alternative round-ups and comparison pages.
- Quote: "the flow builder is overwhelming" (CreatorFlow).

### 5. Billing / cancellation / refund friction  — medium, ManyChat
Charged after cancelling, hard to get refunds; drives the low Trustpilot score.
- Evidence: Trustpilot aggregate, review round-ups.

### 6. Integrations need patchwork  — thin
Connecting to other tools reportedly needs Zapier glue.
- Evidence: one quoted Capterra review. **THIN — single source.**

### Unmatched / notable
- SuperProfile's own help admits "reply delays are normal" and Close-Friends
  content won't trigger — small reliability/clarity gaps, not loud complaints.
- SuperProfile negative reviews are too few (29 on Trustpilot) to theme — **THIN**.

## Read
Everyone competes on features and price. **Reliability + transparency is the
unmet, emotionally-charged job** — and it's cross-product (even SuperProfile has
it). That, plus flat pricing, is the opening.
