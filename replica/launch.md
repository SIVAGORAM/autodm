## Go-to-market — first 100 users (India-first)

The product's wedge is reliability ("know it's working"). Every GTM move should
show that, not just claim it. You already freelance for brands and have a
creator network — use it.

### Phase 0 — before any marketing (gate)
- [ ] Meta App Review approved (else no one can connect — this blocks everything)
- [ ] Reliability features F1–F4 live (the page promises them)
- [ ] 3–5 real automations running on your own / a friend's account for a week, screenshots of the status + delivery log as proof

### Phase 1 — first 10 (warm, this week after the gate)
- Your freelance clients (Tiny Treats, HM Wealth, The Co HR, etc.) — set up one automation each, free. They become your first case studies.
- DM 20 creators you already know: "I built a tool that tells you the second your auto-DM breaks. Want to try it free?"
- Goal: 10 active accounts, 3 testimonials you can quote (with permission).

### Phase 2 — first 100 (India creator channels)
- **Reddit**: r/InstagramMarketing, r/socialmedia, r/IndieHackers — answer "my auto-DM stopped working" threads with genuine help, mention Relayo only when it fits. (This is exactly where the complaints live — see feedback.md.)
- **WhatsApp/Telegram creator groups** (big in India): share a short demo video of the status dashboard catching a break.
- **A reel on your own IG** using your own tool: "I made a tool that DMs people who comment — and shows me it's working." Dogfood = proof.
- **Micro-influencer coaches** (course sellers, 10–50k) — they live on auto-DM and feel reliability pain hardest. Offer Pro free for 3 months for a shoutout.

### Content that sells the wedge (not features)
- "Why your Instagram auto-DM silently stopped (and how to know next time)" — blog + reel. Ranks on the exact pain, ends with Relayo.
- A public status page / uptime number once you have traffic.

### Pricing rollout
- Launch Free + Pro ₹499 flat. Offer founding users an annual lock (₹3,999/yr) to seed cash + retention.
- No paid ads until the funnel converts organically — reliability is a trust sell, best proven, not advertised.

### Metrics to watch (first 60 days)
- Connect-completion rate (OAuth drop-off), first-automation-live rate
- Automations that hit "Needs reconnect" caught by an alert vs. silently (your whole promise)
- Free → Pro conversion, week-4 retention

### Legal before you charge
- Privacy policy + terms (Meta + stores require it), Razorpay KYC, a refund policy.
