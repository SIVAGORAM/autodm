# Fixes & positioning — AutoDM

From `feedback.md`. A straight clone of SuperProfile has no reason to exist;
this is the reason. Three lists, a fix plan, and the angle.

## 1. What they hate (things the tools do)
1. **Automations silently stop firing** — LIVE but dead, often for days, no
   alert. 15+ ManyChat threads; SuperProfile admits reconnect is needed.
   "I am losing followers at this point. This is causing horrible stress."
2. **Bills that grow with your audience** — per-contact pricing; a viral reel
   spikes cost. "the bill grows with your audience" (Tidio).
3. **Support you can't reach when it breaks** — Trustpilot 2.1; "the Get help
   button and support request form are not working for me."
4. **Builder overkill for one simple job** — "the flow builder is overwhelming"
   (CreatorFlow).

## 2. What's missing (asked for by name)
- A way to **know it's working** — a live status/health signal and a delivery
  log, so creators aren't flying blind (implied across every "stopped working"
  thread).
- **Flat, predictable pricing** that doesn't punish a viral moment.
- **Instagram-only simplicity** — comment → DM, done, no flow-chart maze.

## 3. What's unsolved (whole jobs ignored → positioning)
- **Trust that the automation fired.** Nobody sells confidence. Every tool makes
  you discover failure by watching your leads dry up. This is the wedge.
- **The solo Instagram creator** who wants reliability + a flat price, not an
  all-in-one suite (SuperProfile) or an enterprise flow builder (ManyChat).

## Fix plan (top items by evidence × cheapness to build)

| # | Fix | Size | Why (evidence) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | **Automation health + alerts** — detect token/permission expiry and webhook gaps; show a per-automation status (Healthy / Needs reconnect / Paused by Meta) and email the creator the moment it breaks | M | Theme 1, the loudest pain across 15+ threads + SuperProfile's own doc |
| F2 | **Delivery log** — per run: matched / sent / skipped / failed + reason, visible to the creator | S | "nothing happens, no DM, no trigger" — give them the receipt |
| F3 | **Flat, no-contact-cap pricing** — one predictable price, unlimited contacts | S (pricing) | Theme 2; also SuperProfile's own selling point |
| F4 | **Reconnect in one tap** — guided reconnect when the token expires, before leads are lost | S | Theme 1; SuperProfile forces a manual settings reconnect |
| F5 | **One-screen Instagram-only builder** — the 3-step builder you already have, no flow maze | done | Theme 4 — already shipped |
| F6 | **Status page / uptime transparency** — public "is it up" signal | S | Trust is the unsolved job |
| F7 | **Personalization + AI intent** — first-name tags and keyword-free matching | S/M | parity + your LLM edge |

F1, F2, F4, F6 are all the same underlying bet: **make reliability visible.**
That's cheap to build on your existing queue (you already log Runs) and it's
the thing no competitor sells.

## The angle (pick one)

**A — Reliability (recommended)**
> For Instagram creators who've watched their auto-DM quietly die on a viral
> post, AutoDM shows you it's working — live status, a delivery log, and an
> alert the moment it breaks — so you never lose leads to a silent failure.
> Evidence: reliability, 15+ ManyChat threads across forum + blogs + SuperProfile's own docs.

**B — Flat price**
> For creators punished by per-contact pricing, AutoDM is one flat price with
> unlimited contacts — your viral reel doesn't spike your bill.
> Evidence: pricing, G2 + Tidio + Reddit + multiple round-ups.

**C — Instagram-only simplicity**
> For creators who find ManyChat's flow builder overwhelming, AutoDM does one
> thing — comment to DM — in three steps.
> Evidence: complexity, CreatorFlow + comparison pages.

### Recommendation: **A, with B as the proof point.**
Flat pricing (B) is table stakes — SuperProfile and the cheap clones already
have it, so it won't differentiate. Simplicity (C) is crowded. **Reliability +
transparency (A) is the emotionally-charged, unsolved job**, it's cross-product
(even SuperProfile has it), and you can build it cheaply on the Run log you
already have. Lead with "know it's working," back it with flat pricing.

Name/voice should say *dependable*, not *cheap*. Don't put SuperProfile's or
ManyChat's name in your product name or store listing; a factual comparison
page is a lawyer question in your country.
